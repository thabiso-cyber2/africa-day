/Our Story

Who we are

Culture is
our inheritance.
Legacy is our work.

Africa Day is a cultural platform built around the belief that our languages, stories, knowledge and creative expression deserve to be celebrated, protected and carried into the future.

An evolving platform for Africa and its diaspora

Our purpose

A shared space
for identity.

We create platforms where cultural expression meets learning, connection and opportunity.

Africa Day brings together artists, educators, elders, young people, businesses, institutions and communities. It recognises culture as a living body of knowledge: carried in language, music, storytelling, food, craft, memory, land and everyday practice.

The programme is designed to move beyond a single celebration. Public events create visibility and connection; school and community activities create opportunities to learn, document and share; partnerships help strengthen the resources and relationships required for continuity.

“We are not just celebrating. We are writing history through our languages, music and the knowledge we choose to preserve.”

Africa Day · Schools Edition project vision

The 2027 focus on Lesotho @60 brings this vision into a national milestone, with a particular emphasis on language diversity, oral storytelling and digital preservation.

Our guiding principles

The values behind
the platform.

These principles inform the way the platform approaches celebration, education, cultural documentation and collaboration.

01 / RESPECT

Culture with context

Recognise the people, communities and histories behind cultural knowledge, and approach documentation with consent, attribution and care.

02 / CONTINUITY

Knowledge across generations

Create meaningful connections between elders, learners, artists and educators so that knowledge can be shared and carried forward.

03 / ACCESS

Heritage in the digital age

Explore ways to make African-language stories and cultural materials more visible and accessible through digital tools.

04 / PARTNERSHIP

Shared responsibility

Build relationships with schools, institutions, cultural organisations, businesses and diaspora communities.
