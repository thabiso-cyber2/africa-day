The bigger ambition

A celebration that
leaves something behind.

The concept positions 2027 as a “Living Archive”: a programme that celebrates Basotho culture while building a record that can serve future generations.

01 / LANGUAGE

60 stories · 10 districts

The concept proposes producing 60 stories from Lesotho’s 10 districts and preserving Sesotho, SiPhuthi and isiXhosa oral storytelling.

02 / DIGITAL HERITAGE

Culture in the digital space

The programme aims to increase the visibility of African-language stories and cultural knowledge through digital documentation and an archive.

The archive is a proposed legacy initiative. Content governance, permissions, language review, ownership, access and long-term hosting arrangements will need to be agreed with participating communities and partners.
