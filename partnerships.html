Partnership opportunities

Invest in
culture.
Build a legacy.

Partner with Africa Day 2027 to support a multi-day cultural programme and a wider ambition for education, digital heritage, youth participation and cultural exchange.

Lesotho @60 · 25—30 May 2027 · Maseru

Why partner

A visible presence.
A meaningful contribution.

The 2027 concept offers partners several ways to connect with audiences and support a programme spanning arts, education, business, sport, tourism and community.

01 / MULTIPLE TOUCHPOINTS

Six days of engagement

Partner visibility across six programme days, with the concept proposing a lasting presence through the Lesotho @60 Digital Archive.

02 / CROSS-SECTOR REACH

Connected audiences

Engage with arts, education, business, sport, tourism, community groups and diaspora-facing cultural exchange.

03 / NATIONAL SIGNIFICANCE

A milestone year

Connect with the convergence of Africa Day, Heroes Day and Lesotho @60 on the proposed 25 May launch.

04 / CITY & GLOBAL PRESENCE

Local and international

The concept includes an African Market in Maseru CBD and a proposed livestream connection to San Diego.

05 / DEVELOPMENT IMPACT

Learning and opportunity

Support artists, teachers, youth and cultural heritage, alongside knowledge-sharing opportunities for schools.

06 / TAILORED COLLABORATION

Shared objectives

Discuss a partnership that aligns the event’s activities with your organisation’s goals and areas of contribution.

Proposed sponsorship packages

Find your place
in the programme.

The concept proposes the following tiers. Packages can be customised based on partner objectives.

Title Partner — Lesotho @60Naming rights and headline association
M150,000

Naming rights — “Your Company Africa Day Lesotho @60”; all-event branding; Gala speaking slot with Makeda “Dread”; archive co-branding.

Day PartnerOwn one event day
M60,000

Own one event day (for example, “Languages Conference Powered by You”) and receive a panel slot.

Knowledge PartnerSupport learning and masterclasses
M40,000

Sponsor Makeda’s masterclasses and receive logo placement on STEAM kits for 100 learners.

Venue PartnerVenue association
M30,000

Venue branding.

Market PartnerAfrican Market presence
M20,000

African Market branding and two stalls.

Language GuardianSupport a district’s stories
M10,000

Sponsor one district’s six stories and be named guardian of that district’s language in the archive.

Supporting PartnerProgramme support
M5,000

Programme logo placement and tickets.

All amounts are shown in Maloti (M), as presented in the draft sponsorship concept. Benefits, availability, taxes, deliverables and final terms must be confirmed in a written partnership agreement.
